Japanese Language Schools in Tokyo: How to Pick an Affordable One

Tokyo has hundreds of Japanese language schools — from intensive 5-day/week boot camps to 2-evenings-per-week conversation clubs. Here's how to choose without overpaying.

School types at a glance

Type Schedule Cost ballpark Best for
Intensive language school Mon–Fri, 3–5 h/day ¥800,000+/year (visa included) Serious N5→N1 sprint + student visa
Part-time school 2–5 evenings/week ¥15,000–50,000/month Beginners staying long-term
Language café Flexible, pay-per-event ¥2,000–4,000/event Conversation practice, networking
JLPT prep courses Weekend intensives ¥30,000–80,000/level Quick exam boost

Affordable pick in Tokyo: ALA

ALA (アメリカ語学センター/アラ) — after checking many schools in Tokyo, this is one of the more affordable options with solid group courses and a central location.

Use a placement agency — free for you

Agents like Go! Go! Nihon match you with schools, handle the paperwork and visa steps, and cost nothing — the school pays their commission. Worth doing for the visa support alone.

What to check before enrolling

  1. Class size — 8 students max is ideal; 20+ means little speaking time
  2. Textbook system — ask which book; Minna no Nihongo and Genki are standard, trustworthy names
  3. JLPT track record — do they publish pass rates?
  4. Visa support — only relevant if you need one (school-sponsored student visa requires 6+ months)
  5. Level placement — can you enter mid-level, or only at term start?

After school: practice the exam itself

Classes teach Japanese; the JLPT tests a specific format. Both matter.
