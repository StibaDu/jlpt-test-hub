Genki Traveler Review: Travel Health Insurance for Japan

You just landed in Japan. From the moment you clear customs until your ward office processes your NHI registration, you are uninsured — and that gap can last days or weeks. A scooter accident in Shibuya, a bad piece of sashimi, appendicitis at 2 AM — and you are paying ¥100,000+ out of pocket.

Genki Traveler closes that gap in minutes, from your phone, in English. No ward office. No Japanese forms. No waiting.

Genki Traveler — travel health insurance for Japan. Up to 12 months, monthly cancel, all sports included. See current prices →

What Genki Traveler is (in one sentence)

Genki Traveler is a monthly travel health insurance subscription for people abroad: it pays for unexpected medical care — accidents, emergencies, sudden illness — while you travel, up to a total of €1,000,000.

It covers up to 12 months, you pay monthly, and you can cancel after the first month.

What's covered

From Genki's own benefits list:

  • Medical treatment worldwide — doctor visits (outpatient) and hospital stays (inpatient) at public and private hospitals, up to €1,000,000
  • Ambulance emergency transport — road, air, water and rail, to a hospital that fits your medical needs
  • Consultations & telemedicine — including remote doctor advice when you can't easily reach a clinic
  • Prescribed diagnostics & medication — CT, MRI, X-ray, antibiotics, painkillers, crutches, wheelchairs
  • 24/7 assistance & direct billing — if you're hospitalized, Genki arranges and pays the hospital directly, so you don't front the money
  • All sports included — skiing, snowboarding, surfing, diving, hiking, martial arts, scooters. Only professional sport (where you get paid) is excluded
  • Medical repatriation — medically necessary transport back to your home country
  • Hospital visits — one family member can fly to you after a life-threatening emergency (economy class, up to €5,000 per case)
  • Physiotherapy — up to 6 prescribed sessions per case
  • Emergency dental after an accident — basic care up to €1,000 per case

Fast reimbursements: Genki pays 80% of claims within 2 business days; support is available on WhatsApp around the clock.

The catches you should know before you buy

This is the list most reviews skip. It's straight from Genki's own "Good to know":

  • €50 deductible per case. You pay the first €50, insurance covers the rest (no deductible for hospital stays). Dislocated shoulder in Bali at €1,700 → you pay €50.
  • Max 12 months. Travel insurance is not a long-term policy. After it ends you need other cover.
  • 14-day waiting rule. If you sign up while already abroad without prior cover, only emergencies are covered in the first 14 days.
  • Pre-existing conditions excluded. Anything diagnosed, treated or with symptoms in the year before your start date is not covered. Chronic conditions known beforehand are excluded.
  • Home-country cover is limited: emergencies only, first 6 weeks, and only after 4+ weeks abroad. Canada/USA: emergencies only, first 7 days (you can extend for extra cost).
  • Not covered: dental (except accidents), alternative medicine, mental health, maternity, rehab, preventive care. Genki says it plainly: if you want those covered, you want international health insurance — Genki Native.
  • >€1,000 treatments: send an itemized cost estimate at least 5 days in advance, otherwise only 50% reimburses (doesn't apply for hospital stays managed by their assistance team).
  • Insurance ends after repatriation — once you're flown home on their bill, the policy ends there.

How signing up works

  1. You subscribe online — you can already be abroad when you do it
  2. You pay the first month; after that your card is charged monthly
  3. Your cover starts on the date you choose
  4. Cancel anytime after month one — unused days in a billing cycle are refunded automatically
  5. After 12 months the policy ends automatically unless you renew elsewhere

Traveler vs Native — the 10-second version

Genki Traveler = travel insurance: unexpected things, up to 12 months, €1,000,000, sports included, from a low monthly price.

Genki Native = international health insurance: long-term, full cover including dental, mental health, maternity and preventive care, no age limit once you're in.
