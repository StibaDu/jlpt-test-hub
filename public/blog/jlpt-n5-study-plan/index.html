JLPT N5 Study Plan: Your 30-Day Guide

N5 is the easiest JLPT level — but it's also where most people quit, because they study without a structure. Here's a complete 30-day plan that fits 60–90 minutes per day.

What N5 actually requires

Component What you need
Kanji ~100 characters (numbers, days, basic verbs/nouns)
Vocabulary ~800 words
Grammar Particles (は・が・を・に・で・へ・と・も・から・まで), polite forms, past tense, te-form basics
Reading Short passages: emails, notices, simple paragraphs
Listening Slow, clearly spoken dialogues about daily life

The 30-day plan

Days 1–7: Foundations

  • Daily 20 new vocabulary (hiragana words first)
  • 10 kanji per day: numbers 一二三, time words 今日/明日, people/family, basic verbs
  • Grammar: は vs. が, を, に, で — one particle family per day
  • 15 minutes listening: slow YouTube JLPT N5 playlist

Days 8–14: Structure

  • Daily 15 vocab + 10 kanji (review + new)
  • Grammar: verb conjugation — polite -ます → -ました → て-form
  • Start sentence-building: write 5 sentences daily using that day's patterns
  • First practice test (Learning Mode, no pressure)

Days 15–21: Reading & listening push

  • Daily 15 vocab review + 10 kanji
  • Grammar: adjective conjugations (い/な), particles へ, と, も, から, まで
  • Read real JLPT-style passages: 2 short emails/notices daily
  • Listen without pausing: full mock audio sections

Days 22–27: Simulation

  • Two full 30-question tests (Real Test mode, timed)
  • Analyze every mistake — write down the pattern/word, then drill those exact points
  • Flashcards on your mistakes — spaced repetition locks them in

Days 28–30: Final polish

  • Review your entire mistake notebook
  • One final mock test — target ≥70% (the pass bar is 45/60% but leave margin)
  • Sleep, light review only on the last day

Why simulation matters more than study

Most N5 fails aren't knowledge failures — they're format surprises. The real test wraps the same grammar you know in cloze sentences, notices and dialogues, and that unfamiliar shell eats time. Practice tests train the shell, not just the content.
