Avoid these budget traps

⚠️ Skip these if you're on a budget: animal cafés (with a few exceptions), Robot Restaurant and other overly touristy "Cool Japan" venues, maid cafés (expect ~¥2,000 for 30 minutes), and any place that uses people on the street to lure you in.

For panoramic views: Tokyo Skytree costs ¥3,000+ and involves queues — the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building observation deck is free, opens early and late, and the line moves fast.
