# Cheap Eats in Tokyo: 1000+ Restaurants Under ¥1,000

Tokyo has a reputation for being expensive. It isn't — if you know where to look. **Lunch is the secret**: many restaurants in Tokyo run a cheap lunch menu ranging from **¥500 to ¥1,999**, often the same food they serve at dinner for twice the price.

## The ¥1,000 Lunch Rule

> 💡 **Tip: Lunch is cheaper.** Many restaurants in Tokyo have a cheap lunch menu ranging from ¥500 to ¥1,999. So try to get that deal!

We compiled a researched list of **1,000+ Tokyo restaurants with lunch menus under ¥1,000** — izakaya, ramen shops, seafood places, yakitori stands, creative cuisine and more. Every entry includes address, operating hours, payment methods and budget category.

## Avoid these budget traps

> ⚠️ **Skip these if you're on a budget:** animal cafés (with a few exceptions), Robot Restaurant and other overly touristy "Cool Japan" venues, maid cafés (expect ~¥2,000 for 30 minutes), and any place that uses people on the street to lure you in.

For panoramic views: **Tokyo Skytree** costs ¥3,000+ and involves queues — the **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building** observation deck is **free**, opens early and late, and the line moves fast.
